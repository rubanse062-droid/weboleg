Shop talk

Why it pays to stick with one barber

A haircut is cumulative. The barber who knows your head, your growth pattern and your schedule gets it right faster.

A haircut isn't a one-time job — it's a series. Every visit builds on the last one, and the barber who has seen your hair grow out for three months knows things about it that no first-timer can learn in half an hour.

He learns your head

Cowlicks, a crown that spins the wrong way, a spot that grows faster on the left, hair that gets thicker in winter — a regular barber notices these and cuts around them. That's why the second and third cuts from the same barber are usually better than the first.

He remembers what you like

How tight you want the sides, whether you talk or read your phone, that you have to be back at work in forty minutes. A regular barber keeps track. You stop explaining and start just sitting down.

The cut lasts longer

When a barber knows how your hair grows, he can shape a cut so it still looks good in week three, not just on day one. That's the difference between a haircut that "grows out" and one that falls apart.

A style, not a series of experiments

Bouncing between shops gives you a different result every time. With one barber, changes become deliberate: a little more length here, a lower fade next time. If you want to try something new, you're doing it with someone who already knows what will and won't work on your hair.

How to do it at Chico's

Six barbers work at the shop, and you can pick yours every time you book online. Choose a service first, then select your barber in the booking flow. Browse their work in the Meet Your Barber section, find the style closest to what you like, and stick with that chair.

Prices and times are as listed in Chico's online booking menu; check current options when booking.
