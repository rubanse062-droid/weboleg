Beard grooming & shaves · Vancouver, WA

Beard Grooming and Shaves in Vancouver WA

Keep your beard shaped or book a classic shave at Chico's. Choose Beard Trim for clipper work, Full Beard Service for a razor finish, or Hot Razor Shave for a hot towel shave with oils and moisturizing products.

From $30

What to book

Beard Trim is $30 for 30 min. Full Beard Service and Hot Razor Shave are each $40 for 30 min. If you're also getting a haircut, browse the combination services to choose an appointment that covers both.

A beard trim and a full face shave are different services. Select the result you want in the booking menu, or call the shop if you'd like help choosing.

Walk-ins welcome when a barber is available. A card is required to hold your appointment. Running late? Call (360) 896-8159.
