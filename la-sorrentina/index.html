Italian Restaurant & Wood-Fired Pizza in Vancouver

La Sorrentina brings the taste of Southern Italy to Vancouver, Washington. Discover Neapolitan pizza, handmade pasta and Italian classics, with a wood-fired oven at the heart of the kitchen.

From Sorrento to Vancouver, our story is built around a love of Southern Italian cooking. Gather around the table for Neapolitan pizza, pasta and the warmth of our wood-fired kitchen.
