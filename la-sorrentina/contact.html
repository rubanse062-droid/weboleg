BEFORE YOU VISIT

A few helpful details.

Should I reserve a table?

We recommend calling ahead, especially for weekends. Reach our team at (360) 314-4447.

Can I bring my dog?

Dogs are welcome on the outdoor patio during the summer.

Can I customize my meal?

Our menu is served without customizations or substitutions. Please call the restaurant with dietary questions before visiting.

What makes Neapolitan pizza different?

A quick bake in a very hot oven produces a soft, chewy crust. The center can be tender; enjoy it folded, or with a knife and fork.
